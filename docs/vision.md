# Vision

Astro Adventure is a native Apple astronomy adventure for kids ages 4–12. Players explore space, planets, moons, gravity, orbits, and scientific discovery through missions that feel like play first and lessons second.

## Learning Goals

- Build curiosity about astronomy and space science.
- Teach planetary facts through exploration and interaction.
- Introduce physics concepts such as gravity, mass, motion, thrust, and orbits.
- Reinforce learning with quizzes, badges, and replayable challenges.

## Experience Goals

- Delightful on iPhone, iPad, and Apple TV.
- Comfortable with touch, Siri Remote, or a physical controller.
- Readable and understandable for kids, families, and classrooms.
- Adapted for ages 4–6, 7–9, and 10–12 without collecting a birthdate.
- Offline-first, private by default, and free from ads or manipulative purchases.
- Built openly, with license-clean assets and research.

## Product Direction

- Build with Swift, SwiftUI, RealityKit, Game Controller, GameKit, and iCloud technologies.
- Ship the same core missions across iOS, iPadOS, and tvOS with platform-specific controls and layout.
- Design toward Apple Arcade quality while retaining a premium App Store release path.
- Keep all buildable source, content, automation, and redistributable assets public.

# Roadmap

## M0 — Native Apple Parity

Rebuild the Mercury, Mars, and Europa proof using SwiftUI and RealityKit.

Exit criteria:

- iOS and tvOS targets compile from a clean clone.
- Ages 4–6 receive audio-ready text and two-choice discovery checks.
- Ages 7–9 receive short concrete explanations and three-choice checks.
- Ages 10–12 receive richer evidence-based explanations and questions.
- Prompt, navigation, scan, card, quiz, retry, hint, and completion states work.
- Touch and Siri Remote paths work without requiring a physical controller.
- Unit, content, repository, and unsigned app-build checks pass.

## M1 — Input, accessibility, and persistence

- Ship movement and destination focus through touch, Siri Remote, and Game Controller.
- VoiceOver, Switch Control review, reduced motion, optional haptics, captions, and audio controls.
- Versioned local progress and save migration tests.
- iPhone, iPad, and television layout testing.
- First device-based performance budget on the oldest supported hardware.

## M2 — Production vertical slice

- Final visual direction for Signal Sweep.
- Reviewed planet materials, ship model, animation, sound, music, and narration.
- Three polished missions representing the complete production pipeline.
- Game Center achievements and optional private iCloud progress synchronization.
- Internal and external TestFlight testing.

## M3 — Content expansion

- Inner Worlds.
- Giant Planets and Moons.
- Gravity and Orbits.
- Evidence from Spacecraft.
- Review missions adapted to each Explorer Mode.
- Localization-ready text and narration pipeline.

## M4 — Release candidate

- Privacy, accessibility, performance, localization, and App Review audits.
- Store metadata and age-rating review.
- Signed archives and TestFlight release candidate.
- Apple Arcade pitch package and premium App Store fallback plan.

Apple Arcade consideration is external to the engineering milestones and is never treated as guaranteed acceptance.

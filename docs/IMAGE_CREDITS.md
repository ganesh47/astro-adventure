## Educational facts

The flashcard measurements, orbital periods, temperatures and mission numbers
are reviewed against NASA's destination and mission references:

- [Mercury facts](https://science.nasa.gov/mercury/facts/)
- [Mars facts](https://science.nasa.gov/mars/facts/)
- [Europa facts](https://science.nasa.gov/jupiter/moons/europa/europa-facts/)
- [Europa Clipper mission overview](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/europa-clipper/)

Numbers are rounded for an age-appropriate learning experience. Temperature
values refer to representative surface extremes and mission plans may be updated
by NASA as operations progress.
